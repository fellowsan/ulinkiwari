About Us

The Studio Behind the Play

UlinKiwari Games is an indie game studio that believes play is more than just entertainment. We create games that are fun, creative, and close to today's players, while carrying the warmth of Sundanese values and inspiration. Maén di Jaman Kiwari!

Where the Name Comes From

Ulin means to play; kiwari means the present day. Together they describe exactly what we do: making playful things for right now.

We didn't pick the name to sound traditional, we picked it because it says something true. Play is one of the oldest human activities, and it still belongs at the center of modern life.

Our Philosophy

We're guided by ulin bari diajar, the Sundanese idea of learning while playing. A great game teaches without lecturing: it invites curiosity, rewards experimentation, and lets players discover things on their own terms. That's the kind of play we want to make.

Vision

Creating accessible, joyful games for contemporary players, rooted in curiosity and warmth.

Mission
