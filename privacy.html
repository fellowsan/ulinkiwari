Privacy Policy

Owner review required

This page is a template. It must be reviewed and completed by the owner with factual details before it is used as the public policy URL for any Google Play submission. Do not treat it as final legal advice.

1. Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of October 15, 2026 and was last updated on October 15, 2026.

2. Developer Identity and Contact

This policy is provided by UlinKiwari Games ("we", "us"). For privacy-related questions, contact us at support@ulinkiwari.my.id. The developer name shown here should match the developer name used in Google Play Console.

3. Scope

This is the standard privacy policy for all games published by UlinKiwari Games, and for this website (ulinkiwari.my.id).

It applies uniformly to every current and future title, unless a specific game publishes its own separate policy, in that case it will be linked from that game's store listing.

4. Data You Provide

Our games do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play. If you contact us for support (for example by email), we receive your email address and message contents, used solely to respond to your request.

5. Automatically Collected Data

This website does not use analytics or tracking cookies. Our games do not collect data directly to our own servers. Data collection in our games happens through the third-party advertising SDKs described below.

6. Advertising, Analytics, and Third-Party SDKs

Our games are free and supported by advertising. We integrate Google AdMob (by Google LLC) as our advertising SDK. The following data types are collected through AdMob, consistent with our Google Play Data Safety disclosures: (1) Device or other IDs: advertising ID, collected and shared for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention; (2) App activity: ad interactions such as impressions and clicks, collected and shared for advertising and analytics; (3) App info and performance: crash logs and diagnostics, collected and shared for app functionality and analytics; (4) Approximate location derived from IP address, collected and shared for advertising. All data is encrypted in transit. Data is not sold to third parties. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings, Privacy, Ads). You can also reset your advertising ID there. For full details see Google's Privacy Policy. No other third-party SDKs for analytics or crash reporting are used.

7. Purposes of Processing and Data Sharing

Advertising SDK data is used to serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. It is shared with Google (AdMob) as described in their privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Retention and Deletion

We do not store personal data on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete directly. AdMob data is retained per Google's policies. To reset your advertising ID, use your device settings. For other deletion requests, contact us at support@ulinkiwari.my.id.

9. Security

Because we operate no data-collection servers of our own, advertising data security is governed by Google's infrastructure and policies. We take reasonable steps to integrate SDKs only from reputable providers and keep them up to date.

10. Children's Privacy

Our games are general-audience casual games, not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at support@ulinkiwari.my.id. In-game advertising follows Google Play's Families Policy requirements where applicable.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data and to opt out of certain uses. For data held by our advertising partners, use Google's privacy controls. Questions: support@ulinkiwari.my.id.

12. International Data Transfers

Data collected by our advertising partners (Google AdMob) may be processed on servers outside your country, in accordance with Google's data-processing policies and applicable safeguards.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the 'last updated' date above and publish the new version on this page. Continued use of our games after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
