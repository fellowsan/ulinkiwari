Our Story

What does “UlinKiwari” mean?

Ulin = To Play

In Sundanese, ulin means to play. Play is humanity's natural way to learn, connect, and enjoy life, for children and grown-ups alike.

Kiwari = The Present Day

Kiwari means right now, the present day. We make games for today's players: playful, relevant, and easy to pick up.

Put them together and you get our belief: playing is a natural way to learn, connect, and enjoy life. Inspired by ulin bari diajar, learning while playing, we make games that are a joy to play and can leave something meaningful behind.

Inspiration

Ulin Bari Diajar

The Sundanese philosophy of ulin bari diajar, learning while playing, inspires how we work. The warmth of Sundanese stories and values colors our craft, without forcing every game into a traditional theme. Cultural roots inspire us; they don't limit us.
